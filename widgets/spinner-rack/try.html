Try it with your own stock

Open this file straight from your desktop — no server, no install, nothing uploaded anywhere. Everything below stays in this tab.

1 · Your books

Paste straight out of a spreadsheet. Keep your header row and the columns can be in any order — title, author, link, category, cover are recognised under most of their usual names, and anything else is ignored. No header row and it reads them in that order.

Only title is required. category names the panel a book lands on, so four categories fill four facings. It rebuilds as you type.

2 · Your covers

Two ways, and you can mix them: put a cover URL in the cover column above, or drop the image files here. Filenames are matched to titles, so the-salt-path.jpg, The Salt Path.png and salt path 9780241.jpeg all find the same book.

3 · The rack

Drag it, flick it. It ships to phones only, so Phone width is the shape that matters — it pens the rack into 390px, an iPhone with its gutters.

4 · Take it away

When it looks right, copy the markup and paste it into your own template. Dropped covers come out as data: URLs, so swap those for your real image paths on the way.
